# Licence des icones Tabler

*Fichier reecrit par `bin/icones` a chaque execution : ne pas l'editer a la main, editer
`icones/tabler.txt` puis relancer le script.*

Les icones de l'application sous `res/drawable/tabler_*.xml` viennent du paquet npm
[`@tabler/icons`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@tabler/icons) version **3.48.0**
(`icones/package.json`), licence MIT, convertis par
[`svg2vectordrawable`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/svg2vectordrawable).

## Icones converties

- `tabler_arrow_left` — `arrow-left` (outline)
- `tabler_check` — `check` (outline)
- `tabler_x` — `x` (outline)
- `tabler_plus` — `plus` (outline)
- `tabler_chevron_right` — `chevron-right` (outline)
- `tabler_timeline` — `timeline` (outline)
- `tabler_user` — `user` (outline)
- `tabler_movie` — `movie` (outline)
- `tabler_home` — `home` (outline)
- `tabler_trash` — `trash` (outline)
- `tabler_ticket` — `ticket` (outline)
- `tabler_cloud` — `cloud` (outline)
- `tabler_star_filled` — `star-filled` (filled)
- `tabler_sparkles` — `sparkles` (outline)
- `tabler_award` — `award` (outline)
